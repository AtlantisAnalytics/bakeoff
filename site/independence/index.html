Independence

bakeoff.si is a project of Atlantis Analytics, a small independent company. Nobody else pays for it, and no company whose models, engines or hardware we test has any say in what we publish.

Who pays

  • Hardware: every machine that produces results is ours: the 12 GB tier's RTX 3060, the 24 GB tier's RTX 3090 Ti and the 36 GB unified tier's Apple M4 Max. None of it was given or lent to us.
  • Running costs: we pay for the power, the hosting and the judge model's API calls ourselves, on the providers' standard paid or free plans, the same ones anyone can sign up for.
  • No sponsors and no ads. The site has no advertising, no affiliate links and no sponsored placements.

What model makers can and can't do

  • Anyone can suggest a model. We decide what gets tested.
  • No one pays to be tested, sees results before they are published, or can change or remove them.
  • Every model is judged by the same published rules. The rules change only in public, with a new version, never for one model.
  • When we get something wrong, we publish a correction on the week's page and leave the original numbers in the record.

If that ever changes

If we ever accept hardware, a loan of hardware, cloud credits, money or paid work from a company whose models, engines or hardware we test, then:

  • it is listed on this page, with what it was, who gave it and when, before any result it touches is published;
  • every result measured on that hardware or with those credits is labelled as such;
  • the rules, the verdicts and our editorial control stay exactly as they are, and the company gets no preview.

Relationships today

  • AMD AI Developer Program (since 2026-10-18): a free program open to any developer. We have applied for the cloud credits it offers every member; none have been received or used, and no published result comes from them. If they are used, the results will be labelled as above.

Nothing else: no hardware, credits, money or paid work from any company whose models, engines or hardware we test.

AI in our process

Open-ended answers are compared by a judge model, Claude by Anthropic, for information only: judge results never change a pick or a verdict. Anthropic's models are not tested here, and we pay for the API at standard prices.

The harness and this site are written with AI coding assistance. Every change is reviewed and merged by a person, and the code is public.

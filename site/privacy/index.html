Privacy

This site is plain HTML and CSS. It has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts.

The email sign-up

The sign-up form appears on the site once the newsletter is set up. Until then, nothing is collected.

  • What we collect: your email address, and nothing else.
  • Who stores it: our newsletter provider. The form posts directly to them.
  • Double opt-in: you get a confirmation email first, and you are only subscribed once you confirm.
  • Unsubscribing: every email has an unsubscribe link, and it takes effect straight away.
  • Tracking: open and click tracking are switched off.

We use the address only to send the weekly results.
